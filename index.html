ABOUT EDEL REGAN

Where Light Meets the Quiet Solitude of Nature

Edel Regan is an Irish contemporary landscape and fine art painter. Her work explores the shifting moods of natural light across untamed coastlines, ancient woodlands, and quiet country lanes.

Each piece is born from quiet observation—capturing fleeting atmospheres with deliberate texture, oil glazes, and rich chromatic depth. Working from her studio in the West of Ireland, Edel bridges classical realism with expressive, modern brushwork to evoke not merely a place, but the emotional memory of being there.
